About

I'm a data engineer who got here by way of DevOps, which is why I care about the unglamorous parts: CI/CD, testing, lineage, access controls and what happens at 3 a.m. when a pipeline fails.

For the last couple of years that has extended to AI. I've shipped retrieval-augmented generation on Amazon Bedrock, orchestrated agents with LangGraph and MCP, and put evaluation, tracing and guardrails around them so they're safe to run on enterprise data, including HIPAA-regulated healthcare data.

Based in New Jersey, open to relocation.
