Drift: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Drift is a free balloon game. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The app does not require an account, and it does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

Data kept on your device

The app saves your best score and your sound setting on your own device so they are there next time you play. This data never leaves your device, and it is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

Network access and third parties

The game runs entirely on your device. It does not send data to us or to any third party, and it contains no ads or third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

Drift is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
